General Experience and Reflections

Q: What was the most challenging part of this project for you? Were you able to overcome it?
A: I think at first it was a bit confusing having so many different projects and people to work with, the harder part was to maintain frequent contact with them. In some groups I was able to overcome it with more proactivity on my part to reach out, however on others that did not work.

Q: What did you enjoy most about this project?
A: I enjoyed the role as a Lead Developer, unfortunately I did not contribute much in my Junior Developer roles, and I was not able to get anything from my Lead Developer as the Client, but it was fun taking a site plan made by someone else and try my best to match their design idea and also find ways I could exceed expectations.

Q: What would you do differently if you could do the project again?
A: I would like to have had better communication with my client, it would have been fun to meet with him more and identify things we could improve and new design ideas from the development team and from the client as well.

Q: Do you feel more confident in working on real-world projects after this experience? Explain.
Yes, I think it was great to be able to build a site with specifications made by someone else. On the technical side I think it challenged my abilities to be able to build what the client expected, but also to make sure that the team was keeping up with the timeline of the project was something that resembles real-world scenarios very well, in this case I don't think I did a great job at that, but even then being put in that situation pushed me to try and improve on my ability to direct a project and collaborate with others.
